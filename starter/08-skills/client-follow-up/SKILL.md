# Client follow-up

Turn rough call notes into a short follow-up email the client can act on. Same structure every time.

## Steps

1. Read the call notes. Use only what is in them.
2. Write the email in this order:
   - **Subject:** `Follow-up: <topic of the call>`
   - **Greeting:** the client's first name if the notes give it, otherwise `Hi [name],`
   - **One line of thanks** for the call.
   - **What we agreed:** bullets, only decisions that are in the notes.
   - **Next steps:** one bullet per action, as `action - owner - date`. If the notes do not give the owner or the
     date, write `[owner?]` or `[date?]`. Never guess them.
   - **Open points:** questions that are still open. Leave this section out if there are none.
   - **Next contact:** the next call or meeting, only if the notes mention it.
   - **Sign-off:** `Kind regards,` and the sender's name if you know it, otherwise `[your name]`.
3. Check before you answer:
   - At most 150 words, without the subject line.
   - No number, price, date or promise that is not in the notes. If a price or term is unclear, put it under
     open points.
   - Same language as the notes. Plain words, no exclamation marks, no emojis.

## Example

Notes: "Call with Alpha Retail (example). Tom. They want the fuel surcharge explained. I send the calculation.
Next call after they read it."

Email:

Subject: Follow-up: fuel surcharge

Hi Tom,

Thanks for the call today.

What we agreed:
- I explain how the fuel surcharge is calculated.

Next steps:
- Send the fuel surcharge calculation - me - [date?]

Next contact: a call after you have read the calculation.

Kind regards,
[your name]
