This project is for contract renewals at Example Logistics.

PRICES
- Take every price from the price list file. Name the client, the service, the unit and the 2027 price.
- If a price is not in the file, say "not in the price list". Never estimate a price.
- Show the change from 2026 to 2027 in EUR and in %.

DATES
- A contract that ends within 60 days: mark it "renewal due".

OUTPUT
- Answer in one line first. Then a short table if there is more than one service.
