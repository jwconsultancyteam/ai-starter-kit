First three questions for Claude Code
Builder, step 13

Use a test folder with only shipments-export-sample.csv in it.
Press Shift + Tab until the bottom line says "plan mode on". Then ask one question at a time.

1. What is in this folder? Answer in three short lines.

2. What does shipments-export-sample.csv contain? Name the columns and which weeks it covers.

3. Do not change or create any file. Which three routes changed most in pallets between the last two weeks in the file?

Check the answers yourself:
- 198 rows, 7 columns: date, shipment_id, client, route, pallets, weight_kg, status
- Weeks 35 to 42 of 2026, from 24 August to 16 October
- Four routes: NL-BE, BE-NL, BE-FR and BE-BE
- Week 41 to week 42: BE-BE +35, NL-BE +24, BE-FR -23 (the same three as step 11)

If an answer is wrong, ask: "Which lines of the file did you read for that answer?"
