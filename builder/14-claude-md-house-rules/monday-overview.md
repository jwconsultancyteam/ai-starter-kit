# Context pack: the Monday overview

Who reads it: the planning team, on Monday at 08:00, in two minutes.

Output, in this order:
1. Your first line is the table. No title or sentence before it.
2. The table: route, last week, this week, change in pallets. Biggest change first, up or down.
3. At most 3 short lines after the table: the biggest rise, the biggest drop, and anything missing from the file.

Done when: the table and at most 3 lines. Nothing else.
